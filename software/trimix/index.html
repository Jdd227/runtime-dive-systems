Verification before filling

LIMITATIONS

This release uses ideal-gas pressure addition at a single, uniform temperature. It does not correct for real-gas compressibility, fill heating/cooling, pressure-gauge error, source bank limitations, or regulator/boosting losses. High-pressure helium blends can differ materially from the predicted composition.

Use this build only to compare calculations. Do not use it as the sole basis for live filling. Qualified blending procedures, appropriate oxygen-compatible equipment and air, cylinder limits, and independent O₂/He analysis remain essential.

Depth and gas analysis

OC / CCR
Gas density uses 0°C reference densities. CCR loop model assumes maintained setpoint and inert gas proportions derived from diluent. It is not a dynamic loop simulation.

END estimates are provided with O₂ + N₂ narcotic and N₂-only assumptions. Density thresholds are 5.2 g/L preferred and 6.3 g/L upper guideline per BSAC; they are not stand-alone gas-selection criteria.

Temperature pressure correction

Ideal gas / fixed amount
Uses absolute pressure and Kelvin. Cylinder and gas must reach uniform temperature for a reliable settled reading.

Temperature compensation here is ideal-gas only. It does not turn the blending engine into a real-gas model, and does not model the transient temperature during He or oxygen addition.
