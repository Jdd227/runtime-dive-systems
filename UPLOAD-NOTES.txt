TO UPLOAD TO GITHUB
1. Unzip this package on your Mac.
2. Open the repository linked above, choose Add file > Upload files.
3. Drag the extracted assets and content folders (not the enclosing ZIP folder) to GitHub.
4. Confirm these paths are preserved when uploading, then commit to main:
   assets/designs/genesis-dpv-stand.stl (new)
   assets/designs/genesis-dpv-stand-end-cap.stl (new)
   assets/site.js (updated, adds stand links without removing other downloads)
   content/products.json (updated, describes three parts)
5. Check Actions > Publish RunTime for success, then refresh the stand design page.
